# DDR-001: Original IP, Inspired by Mirrorworld

* **Status:** Accepted
* **Date:** 2026-11-08
* **Resolves:** Q1 (Direct fan adaptation vs. original IP)

## Context

The concept started as a dark reinterpretation of Cornelia Funke's *Mirrorworld* (*Reckless*). Keeping her terminology (*Goyl, Kami'en, Austrien, the Tailor*) would limit the project to a non-commercial fan work.

Most of the concept is already original: the Schieferwald, the Flayer, the Court of Rot, the Smelter Syndicate, the Mirror-Cutters and the Chained Mirror. The grimdark body-horror tone is also far from the source. Keeping canon names would add little and block every commercial path.

## Decision

**Schieferwald is an original IP.** Mirrorworld is credited as an inspiration only. No names, characters, places, invented terms or distinctive character concepts from the novels are used.

### What stays (shared folklore, public domain)

These come from European folk tales and the Grimm collections, not from Funke:

* A mirror as a gateway between worlds
* Witches, the gingerbread house (*Knusperhaus*), changelings, tailors, cursed garments
* Glass slippers, spindles, enchanted skins, petrification curses
* A fairy-tale world colliding with early industrialization (a broad genre premise, not a specific expression)

### What is replaced

| Mirrorworld element | Schieferwald replacement | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Goyl** (stone-skinned people) | **The lithified** (colloquially *Slates*) | A curse you catch, not a race you're born into. See below. |
| Jade, onyx, carnelian, moonstone skin | **Slate first**, plus local valley minerals (e.g. schist, ironstone, flint) | Ties the curse to the valley's geology and avoids the Goyl palette. Replaces "slate, onyx, or jade" from the original spec. |
| **Kami'en** (Goyl king) | Not replaced | The bible needs no stone-skinned monarchy. |
| **Austrien, Albion, Lothringen** and other nations | Placeholders: *the Crown*, *the Empire*, *the wider war* | Name the outside world only if the story needs it, and invent the names fresh. |
| **The Tailor** | **The Flayer** | Already original. Keep the Grimm "tailor" motif general and avoid the novel character's specifics. |
| Dark Fairy, Red Fairy, Alderelves | **The Court of Rot** and its members | Use no fairy hierarchy taken from the novels. |
| Jacob Reckless, Will | **The unnamed outcast** | No brothers-across-worlds premise. |

### Risk flag: the fox companion (Q4)

A **shapeshifting fox girl companion** is the most recognizable character concept in the novels (*Fox*). If a fox companion stays, it must clearly differ in nature, origin and role. For example: a creature that is not a human shapeshifter, a fox spirit with its own agenda, or a different animal entirely. Resolve this as part of Q4.

## Alternatives Considered

* **Direct fan adaptation:** keeps the canon names. Rejected because it limits the project to non-commercial portfolio use, adds little since the content is already mostly original, and would force the tone to stay faithful to the source.
* **Hybrid (canon names now, rename later):** rejected. Renaming late spreads inconsistencies through every document and through the concept-art prompts.

## Consequences

* Every document uses only the names in this DDR or names invented later.
* `README.md` credits Mirrorworld as an inspiration and states there is no affiliation.
* The original spec (`project_handover_repository_specification.md`) still contains canon terms. It is archived as a historical source, not canon.
* Concept-art prompts must avoid describing Funke characters, especially jade-skinned figures and a fox-girl companion.
* New names should come from German folklore, mining and smithing vocabulary, and the valley's geology, which fits the Schieferwald.
